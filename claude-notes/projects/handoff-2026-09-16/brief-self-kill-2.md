=== A. THE OWNER'S DESIGN (binding) ===
(a) The kill credential IS the target's exit payload at -1: "the process gname resource already tracks Q, and Q(-1) becomes the precondition for kill() of that PID". No separate credential predicate, no alive token. (b) A process supplies Q(-1) when it may trap into the kernel at a KILLING cause (a vmfault, an illegal instruction, an ecall instruction whose fetch faults) and supplies the syscall precondition, not Q(-1), when it enters with the syscall cause; hence no separation-logic ∧ and no -1 wand at ecalls. (c) `killed()` reports the flag; the payload rides in the process's private state while the flag is set and the process has not exited, and is consumed ONCE at exit. (d) `riscv_kill_cred` on the fixed record survives ONLY as the APPLICATION'S TAINT -- the antecedent of each process's published payment wand `□ (riscv_kill_cred -∗ Q (-1))` and the price a THIRD-PARTY killer pays (`SpecKkill`/`SpecSysKill` keep `□ riscv_kill_cred -∗`). ONE EXCEPTION, blessed by the coordinator pending the owner's word: the forking process supplies its child's persistent taint wand `□ (riscv_kill_cred -∗ sfork_pay f (-1))` at the fork ecall (`SpecUsertrap.v:~728`, `SpecKfork.v:~378`, `SpecAllocproc.v:~369`) -- free at `Q = fun _ => True` and the only -1 wand at an ecall.
