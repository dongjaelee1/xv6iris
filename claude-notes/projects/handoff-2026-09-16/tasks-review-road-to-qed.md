### 4. THE POST-QED REDESIGN

*One IO resource.* `riscv_out_res`, `riscv_in_res` and `riscv_win_res` are three views of one thing: the console's I/O history `(acc, pops, dl)` with one ghost per era. Replace them by one field `riscv_cons_res : nat -> list mobs -> cons_hist -> iProp` held at ONE witness in `uart_inv`, with one link family `cons_link k h (ev : cons_event) Φ` where `ev ∈ {Out b, In c cs, Read ws}`; the echo shift becomes one fupd per accepted byte (`In c cs` files the entry and the echoes atomically), which deletes the chain-first window, `ein_pend`, `wcnt`, `app_win`, `win_at` and the PLIC carry. The window token exists only because the kernel's shift is split (`in_run = in_append ∧ echo_link`) and `□`; a single atomic event makes it unnecessary.

*The app–kernel interface.* Today the record has eleven Σ-level predicates and a dozen "the ambient field IS this record's" equations (`Hinit_boot`, `Happ_echo`). The honest shape is: the kernel is parametric in ONE application structure `A` whose fixed part is *instantiated once* at `boot_fixedGS`, and every per-era linear thing (turn, token, seeds) is minted by ONE step (`Hpow`'s on-arm) and delivered by ONE carrier (`power_boot_res` → init's bundle), never by the PLIC. The taint should stop being a machine field (`riscv_kill_cred`): the kill price is `Q(-1)` (owner's ruling); the application's "everything is free once tainted" is a property of its own predicates, expressible as `Happ_taint : app_sup ⊢ □ (∀ …, out/in/win are derivable)` without a fixed-record slot.

*Shape now so removal is cheap:* (1) keep `ewin`/`wcnt` confined to `EchoOut.v` and `app_win` to one carry — do not let IO-LEAF or SH-LINE mention `riscv_win_res`; (2) the `dl_cnt` half (W3 fix) is application-owned and survives the merge; (3) state the four kernel rows (W2, W4, W5) as facts about the C, not about the application — they survive; (4) do NOT put the prologue alternatives into `App.v` — EchoDisc only; (5) the turn's transport (`Rc`, `Pay`, exit payload) is WAIT-EXIT's and survives. What the redesign would have to UNDO at cost: the three-field `Htx` signature (App.v:395-470) and its forty carriers, and `Hinit_boot`'s equation list — accept them now, but keep every new obligation a one-line addition in the same style so a single mechanical rewrite removes them.
